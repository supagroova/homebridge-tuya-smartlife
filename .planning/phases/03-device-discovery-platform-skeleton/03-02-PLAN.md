<threat_model>
## Risks

- Placeholder accessories could clutter HomeKit and confuse users.
- Incorrect UUID derivation could duplicate accessories on restart.
- Incorrect pruning could remove valid accessories after a failed discovery.

## Mitigations

- Registration tests cover unsupported skip and cached reuse.
- Discovery errors are handled in Plan 03-01/03-03 before pruning runs.
- UUID and prune behavior are deterministic tests.
</threat_model>
