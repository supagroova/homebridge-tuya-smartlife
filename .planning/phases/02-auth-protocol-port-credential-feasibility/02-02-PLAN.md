<threat_model>
## Risks

- Logging or thrown errors could expose token material.
- Concurrent refresh races could invalidate saved tokens or spam Tuya.
- Mock tests could assert less than the signed wire contract actually needs.

## Mitigations

- Redaction is covered by tests.
- Single in-flight refresh behavior is explicitly tested.
- Nock assertions verify headers and encrypted payload shape, not just high-level return values.
</threat_model>
